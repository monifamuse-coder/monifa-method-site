Good to Know

Questions, answered.

What do I actually get when I check out?

A PDF, and — where relevant — a private link to listen. Both are delivered straight to your inbox via Beacons, ready whenever you are.

Is this a physical book?

No — everything in the Archive is digital only: a PDF to read, and audio to listen to on any phone, tablet, or computer.

I'm new to this. Is it too advanced for me?

No experience needed. Everything's written in plain language, and each piece stands on its own — you don't need to have read the others first.

Do I have to buy the bundle, or can I start with one ebook?

Either way. Start with a single ebook and add the bundle later, or the reverse — nothing here locks you into one path.

Does every ebook come with an audiobook version?

Not yet — currently The Power of Detachment includes both formats. More audio versions are being added to the Archive over time.

What if it's not right for me — is there a refund?

Ebooks and audiobooks are delivered instantly, so those are non-refundable. The Money Foundation Trio and The Faceless Studio are different — both come with a 7-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked.

Who is Muse?

Muse is a fully AI-generated persona — the voice and face behind this work. What she writes and says is real; how she's delivered is built, and that's disclosed openly, never hidden.
