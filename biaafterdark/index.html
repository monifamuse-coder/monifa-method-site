Who is Bia

Bia is the voice you hear after dark. She sings in three languages because that is how the songs arrived: one feeling, said three ways.

The songs are about being held, taking your time and feeling safe enough to fall. Each one is built for a late hour, a low light and a body that wants to slow down.

BIA is a 100% AI-generated persona. Every track is real. The voice delivering it is built, and the words, the sound and every decision behind it are directed by a human who stays out of frame.
